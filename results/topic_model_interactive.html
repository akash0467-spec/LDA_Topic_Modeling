Topic Number Optimization (Coherence & Log Perplexity)

Different numbers of topics (3, 5, 7, and 10) were evaluated to determine the optimal balance between thematic granularity and semantic coherence. The 7-topic model achieved peak coherence ($C_v = 0.4985$).
